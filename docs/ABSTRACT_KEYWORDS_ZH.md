## 1. 英文标题、摘要与关键词

### Title
Directional Error Drift and Diagnostic Concordance in Computerized Dietary Tracking Across Global Food Cultures: A Tri-Cohort Safety Evaluation

### Running Title
Dietary AI Concordance Across Cuisines

### Abstract
Mobile meal analysis algorithms promise automated objective tracking for dietary epidemiology and cardiometabolic therapy, yet established models remain trained predominantly on homogeneous Western workplace cafeteria food. Transferring these image networks to diverse cultural food traditions introduces unexamined measurement errors that can jeopardize glycemic control and clinical insulin titration. To quantify these risks, an Atwater energy-constrained convolutional network was systematically benchmarked across 3,823 standardized plate exposures representing three global dietary domains: Nutrition5k ($N=506$ Western cafeteria servings), FoodBD ($N=1,833$ traditional South Asian curried dishes), and ECUSTFD ($N=1,484$ East Asian plated meals). Cross-domain estimation accuracy was appraised using medical agreement limits, Lin concordance statistics, and American Diabetes Association prandial dosing thresholds ($\pm 15$\,g or $\pm 20\%$ margin). Within the Western training domain, the algorithm produced well-centered carbohydrate estimates ($+0.94$\,g average bias, $p = 0.176$), maintaining an acceptable $63.8\%$ clinical safety rate. In sharp contrast, direct evaluation on South Asian preparations provoked profound negative carbohydrate undercounting (mean error $-18.18$\,g, $p = 8.64 \times 10^{-206}$, Lin concordance coefficient $0.018$), eroding clinical dosing compliance to $49.5\%$ and leaving $50.5\%$ of servings in a hazardous insulin under-allocation zone. For East Asian selections, cafeteria-derived assumptions produced substantial positive energy inflation ($+134.7$\,kcal bias, $p < 10^{-300}$). Analytical error partitioning revealed that undetected nutrient densities hidden beneath optically occluded surfaces accounted for exceeding $75\%$ of cross-cultural variance, overshadowing 2D boundary segmentation discrepancies. Localized model adjustment with only 50 cohort-specific images recovered clinical target compliance to $63.9\%$ and decreased energy estimation error by $39.6\%$. These outcomes demonstrate that convolutional intake monitors incorporate implicit regional preparation biases that compromise clinical safety, establishing multi-cohort cross-cultural auditing and local adaptation pipelines as mandatory requirements for reliable digital nutrition surveillance.

### Keywords
dietary intake assessment, cross-cultural generalizability, systematic estimation bias, deep learning, carbohydrate counting, algorithmic fairness, type 2 diabetes mellitus

## 2. 中文标题、摘要与关键词

### 标题
计算机辅助膳食监测在不同饮食文化下的方向性误差偏移与诊断一致性：一项三队列安全性评估

### 简明页眉标题
膳食人工智能在不同烹饪传统中的一致性

### 摘要
移动端餐食分析算法有望为膳食流行病学调查与心血管代谢疾病干预提供客观、自动化的摄入监测，但现有模型主要基于同质化的西方工作场所食堂配餐进行训练。将此类图像网络推广至不同文化的膳食体系时，会引入未经系统检验的测量误差并威胁患者餐时胰岛素滴定的临床安全性。为系统量化此类风险，本研究基于涵盖 3,823 张标准化进餐图像的三队列基准（含 506 份 Nutrition5k 西方食堂餐盘、1,833 份 FoodBD 传统南亚咖喱餐食以及 1,484 份 ECUSTFD 东亚独立菜肴），系统评估了受 Atwater 生理能量守恒约束的卷积网络的跨域泛化能力。跨域估算精度通过医学一致性界限、Lin 一致性相关系数以及美国糖尿病协会制定的餐时用药安全边界（±15 g 或 ±20% 相对误差）展开综合检验。在西方源域基准中，算法展现出无显著偏倚的碳水化合物估算表现（平均偏差 +0.94 g，p = 0.176），临床安全达标率达 63.8%。与此形成鲜明对比的是，直接评估南亚餐食时诱发了严重的负向碳水化合物少算（平均偏差 -18.18 g，p = 8.64 × 10^-206，Lin 一致性系数 0.018），使临床给药合规率跌落至 49.5%，导致 50.5% 的进餐样本处于胰岛素给药不足的危险区间。而在东亚菜肴中，食堂先验则导致了显著的正向能量虚高（+134.7 kcal，p < 10^-300）。解析性误差分解显示，光学遮挡基质内部未被感知的营养密度差异贡献了超过 75% 的跨文化变异，其影响远超二维轮廓分割误差。仅使用 50 份目标队列样本进行就地自适应调整，即可使临床达标率回升至 63.9%，并将能量估算误差抑制 39.6%。上述结果表明卷积膳食监测系统内嵌了区域性的烹饪制备偏见并危及临床安全，确立了多队列跨文化审计与就地自适应管线在保障数字化营养监测可靠性中的必要地位。

### 关键词
膳食摄入评估, 跨文化泛化性, 系统性估算偏差, 深度学习, 碳水化合物计算, 算法公平性, 2型糖尿病
