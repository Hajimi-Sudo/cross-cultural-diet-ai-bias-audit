## 1. 英文标题、摘要与关键词

### Title
Measurement Reliability and Directional Disparity in Deep Learning Dietary Tracking Across Culinary Traditions: A Tri-Cohort Clinical Safety Assessment

### Running Title
Culinary Disparity in Dietary AI

### Abstract
Automated food photography provides compelling capabilities for digital nutritional surveillance and diabetes self-care, yet existing deep regression architectures are optimized almost exclusively on North American institutional dining plates. When applied to non-Western dietary patterns, optical regression systems encounter hidden compositional shifts that can compromise patient safety. Here, we examined the transportability and directional error profiles of an Atwater-regularized convolutional model across 3,823 standardized eating instances drawn from three distinct eating cultures: Nutrition5k ($N=506$ institutional American trays), FoodBD ($N=1,833$ authentic South Asian mixed recipes), and ECUSTFD ($N=1,484$ East Asian individual dishes). Out-of-domain predictions were quantified using limits of agreement, concordance coefficients, and clinical bolusing boundaries ($\pm 15$\,g and $\pm 20\%$) formulated by the American Diabetes Association. On familiar Western dishes, carbohydrate estimations showed negligible systematic skew ($+0.94$\,g mean deviation, $p = 0.176$), with $63.8\%$ falling within permissible therapeutic boundaries. Direct application to South Asian dining, however, elicited substantial negative carbohydrate underestimation (mean discrepancy $-18.18$\,g, $p = 8.64 \times 10^{-206}$, Lin concordance $0.018$), which depressed therapeutic acceptability to $49.5\%$ and left more than half of meal events vulnerable to insulin under-dosing. When confronted with East Asian preparations, the algorithm exhibited marked caloric overestimation ($+134.7$\,kcal divergence, $p < 10^{-300}$), driven by mismatched portion density expectations. Orthogonal error apportionment indicated that concealed nutrient densities within opaque sauces explained over $75\%$ of cross-dataset variation, vastly surpassing the contribution of planar contour segmentation inaccuracies. Targeted adaptation using 50 regional samples raised clinical compliance back to $63.9\%$ while diminishing caloric error by $39.6\%$. Collectively, these findings demonstrate that optical dietary models embed culture-specific culinary priors that undermine nutritional monitoring safety, establishing multi-ethnic validation protocols and localized fine-tuning as prerequisites for equitable deployment in global digital healthcare.

### Keywords
dietary intake assessment, cross-cultural generalizability, systematic estimation bias, deep learning, carbohydrate counting, algorithmic fairness, type 2 diabetes mellitus

## 2. 中文标题、摘要与关键词

### 标题
面向不同烹饪传统的深度学习膳食监测测量可靠度与方向性偏差：一项三队列临床安全评估

### 简明页眉标题
膳食人工智能中的烹饪文化偏差

### 摘要
智能手机餐食摄影为数字化营养监测与糖尿病居家管理带来了全新契机，但现有的深度回归架构几乎全部基于北美机构食堂配餐盘进行参数优化。当此类算法应用于非西方饮食模式时，会遭遇未被表征的内部组分迁移并危及患者用药安全。本研究基于涵盖 3,823 个标准化进餐实例的三队列基准（含 506 份 Nutrition5k 美式食堂餐盘、1,833 份 FoodBD 南亚传统复合食谱以及 1,484 份 ECUSTFD 东亚独立菜品），系统评估了受 Atwater 约束的卷积模型在跨文化迁移时的表现与方向性偏差分布。域外预测性能通过医学一致性界限、一致性相关系数以及美国糖尿病协会制定的临床用药阈值（±15 g 与 ±20%）进行量化。在熟悉的西方菜肴基准测试中，碳水化合物估算展现出极小的系统偏倚（平均偏差 +0.94 g，p = 0.176），达标率达到 63.8%。然而直接推断南亚餐食时诱发了严重的负向低估（平均偏差 -18.18 g，p = 8.64 × 10^-206，Lin 一致性系数 0.018），使治疗可接受度骤降至 49.5%，半数以上进餐事件面临胰岛素给药不足的临床风险。在应对东亚菜品时，模型由于密度先验失配表现出显著的热量高估（+134.7 kcal，p < 10^-300）。正交误差分解表明，不透明汤汁与糊状基质内部隐匿的营养密度差异解释了超过 75% 的跨数据集方差，其影响远超平面轮廓分割误差。利用 50 份区域样本进行靶向自适应微调后，临床合规率成功回升至 63.9%，热量偏差削减了 39.6%。上述发现共同证实光学膳食模型固化了特定饮食文化的烹饪先验从而削弱了营养监测安全性，确立了多民族验证协议与就地微调校准作为全球数字医疗系统公平部署的前提条件。

### 关键词
膳食摄入评估, 跨文化泛化性, 系统性估算偏差, 深度学习, 碳水化合物计算, 算法公平性, 2型糖尿病
