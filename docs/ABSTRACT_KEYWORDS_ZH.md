## 1. 英文标题、摘要与关键词

### Title
Diagnostic Agreement and Directional Bias of Deep Vision Dietary Intake Estimators Across Varied Food Traditions: A Tri-Domain Nutritional Audit

### Running Title
Dietary Vision Bias Across Traditions

### Abstract
Optical dietary logging offers scalable opportunities for nutritional epidemiology and glycemic management, yet deep regression models are parameterized predominantly on North American institutional cafeteria plates. Extrapolating these algorithms to diverse food cultures risks uncharacterized measurement drift that compromises clinical decision-making. Here, we audited the transportability and systematic bias of an Atwater-constrained convolutional network across 3,823 standardized eating scenes from three cohorts: Nutrition5k ($N=506$ Western plates), FoodBD ($N=1,833$ South Asian composite preparations), and ECUSTFD ($N=1,484$ East Asian discrete dishes). Estimates were benchmarked against limits of agreement, concordance coefficients, and diabetic bolusing boundaries of $\pm 15$\,g and $\pm 20\%$. Within the Western baseline, carbohydrate estimates showed negligible skew ($+0.94$\,g, $p = 0.176$), maintaining $63.8\%$ clinical safety compliance. Direct transfer to South Asian dining provoked severe negative carbohydrate underestimation (mean bias $-18.18$\,g, $p = 8.64 \times 10^{-206}$, Lin concordance $0.018$), collapsing clinical compliance to $49.5\%$ and leaving over half of meals vulnerable to insulin under-dosing. On East Asian items, cafeteria density priors induced marked positive caloric divergence ($+134.7$\,kcal, $p < 10^{-300}$). Variance decomposition indicated that concealed nutrient densities within opaque gravy matrices explained exceeding $75\%$ of cross-dataset discrepancy, far surpassing planar segmentation error. Target-domain recalibration with 50 localized samples restored clinical compliance to $63.9\%$ and diminished caloric error by $39.6\%$. These findings reveal that dietary vision algorithms embed regional preparation assumptions that undermine patient safety, establishing multi-cohort cross-cultural auditing and localized recalibration as essential requirements for equitable digital health.

### Keywords
dietary intake assessment, cross-cultural generalizability, systematic estimation bias, deep learning, carbohydrate counting, algorithmic fairness, type 2 diabetes mellitus

## 2. 中文标题、摘要与关键词

### 标题
深度视觉膳食摄入估算器在不同饮食传统中的诊断一致性与方向性偏差：一项三领域营养学审计

### 简明页眉标题
膳食视觉模型在不同饮食传统中的偏差

### 摘要
光学膳食记录为营养流行病学调查与血糖管理带来了可扩展的应用机遇，但当前的深度回归网络主要基于北美机构食堂配餐盘进行参数优化。将此类算法外推至多元饮食文化时，会面临未经系统表征的测量漂移风险并危及临床决策安全。本研究基于涵盖 3,823 个标准化进餐场景的三队列基准（含 506 份 Nutrition5k 西方餐盘、1,833 份 FoodBD 南亚传统复合食谱以及 1,484 份 ECUSTFD 东亚独立菜肴），系统审计了受 Atwater 生理约束的卷积网络的跨域迁移性与系统性偏差分布。模型输出结果对照医学一致性界限、一致性相关系数以及正负 15 g 与 20% 的糖尿病餐时用药安全边界展开全面检验。在西方基准测试中，碳水化合物估算展现出极小的系统偏倚（平均偏差 +0.94 g，p = 0.176），临床安全合规率保持在 63.8%。直接推断南亚餐食时诱发了严重的负向碳水化合物低估（平均偏差 -18.18 g，p = 8.64 × 10^-206，Lin 一致性系数 0.018），使临床合规率跌落至 49.5%，导致半数以上进餐样本面临胰岛素给药不足的临床风险。而在东亚菜品上，食堂密度先验则引发了显著的正向热量虚高（+134.7 kcal，p < 10^-300）。方差正交分解表明，不透明汤汁与复合糊状基质内部隐匿的营养密度差异解释了超过 75% 的跨数据集差异，其影响远超平面轮廓分割误差。仅使用 50 份区域样本进行就地自适应重校准，即可使临床达标率回升至 63.9%，并将能量估算误差降低 39.6%。上述实证发现揭示了视觉膳食算法内嵌了特定区域的烹饪制备偏见并削弱了患者安全，确立了多队列跨文化审计与就地重校准作为推动全球数字医疗公平性与可靠性的必要路径。

### 关键词
膳食摄入评估, 跨文化泛化性, 系统性估算偏差, 深度学习, 碳水化合物计算, 算法公平性, 2型糖尿病
